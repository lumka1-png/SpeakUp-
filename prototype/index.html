SpeakUp! prototype

A clickable preview of the full lesson loop: pick 10 words, play 3 vocabulary games, speak, listen, and get your score.

What works here

  • 4 TOEIC levels and 8 themed lists (240 words with Traditional Chinese)
  • Word selection, My Words, and practice from saved words
  • All three games, the speaking step, the podcast, and the quiz
  • Scores, stars, XP, streaks, and word mastery, saved in this browser
  • Beta word reports: tap the flag to report a wrong translation, definition, or example

What is simulated

  • Speaking feedback is rule-based. The real app sends your sentence to Claude.
  • The podcast script comes from a template and plays with your browser's built-in voices. The real app has Claude write a new episode for each lesson.
  • This preview window may block the microphone. Type your sentence instead, or open the HTML file directly in Chrome or Safari to record.
